A home for the links you keep.

Linkme is a local-first link manager for Windows. Capture a link in seconds, give it a place, and find it again from any app.

Get Linkme for Windows Read the privacy policy

From clipboard to collection

Capture

Copy a URL and press Ctrl Shift L. Choose a category and add a title, or use Quick-save to send it straight to Inbox.

Organize

Arrange links in categories and groups. Add notes, pin important categories, and keep your library available offline.

Find again

Press Ctrl Shift K to search titles, URLs, and notes in a floating window. Open a result in your default browser.

Optional Google Drive backup

Linkme uses your Google account identifier to recognize the connected account. It displays your Google profile name and photo if available. Drive access lets Linkme create, list, restore, and manage its own backup files in your Drive.

Automatic backups are off when you first connect. Choose Back up now or enable a schedule to start. You can keep using your local library without connecting Google.
