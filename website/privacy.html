Linkme privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy covers the Linkme Windows desktop app, its optional Google Drive backup feature, and this product website. Contact the developer at machekelsallemi@gmail.com.

Your local library

Linkme stores saved URLs, titles, subtitles, notes, categories, groups, and settings in SQLite in your Windows user's application data. Linkme reads your clipboard when you invoke Capture. It uses copied URLs for the requested capture, according to your selected capture mode. The core library works offline without a Linkme account.

Google account information and permissions

When you choose to connect, Linkme opens your default browser for Google's consent flow. Google handles your sign-in. Linkme receives authorization tokens and reads your Google account's unique identifier, profile name, and profile picture if available. The identifier helps recognize the connected account, and the name and picture show which account is connected. The app does not request access to your Google email address, contacts, Gmail, or all of your Drive files.

openid

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file
Use per-file Drive access to create the Linkme backup folder and snapshots, list and read eligible snapshots for restore, and move eligible older snapshots to Drive Trash.

Linkme uses this Google data to operate the account connection and the backup and restore features you choose. Linkme does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Linkme's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Backups, retention, and restore

Automatic backups are off when you first connect. To upload, choose Back up now or enable a schedule. Reconnecting the same account preserves its existing schedule. Linkme sends a SQLite snapshot directly to your Google Drive. Snapshots include the library and SQLite preferences, so they can contain private URLs and notes. The Google authentication session is stored separately and is excluded from the snapshot. New snapshots go under Linkme Backups in My Drive. Eligible older Linkme backups stored at the Drive root can also be listed for restore.

The selected policy keeps up to seven manual or daily copies, four weekly copies, or twelve monthly copies among eligible backups from that installation. Automatic cleanup follows a confirmed upload and a complete listing. It moves older eligible, account-owned, unshared backups tagged for this installation to Drive Trash. Linkme does not empty the Trash. You can use Google Drive to delete or recover files under Google's retention rules.

To restore, choose an available snapshot and confirm replacement. Linkme downloads and validates the snapshot, saves a local recovery copy, and replaces the local links, categories, and groups. Device preferences and the Drive connection stay in place. Local recovery files may remain until you remove them.

Security and local retention

The Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) protects the stored Google refresh token, profile, and backup schedule for your Windows user. Linkme uses HTTPS for Google API calls. The local SQLite library, local recovery copies, and Drive backup files are readable SQLite data. Linkme does not encrypt those files. Access depends on your Windows account and device protections, and on your Google Drive access and sharing settings.

Your library remains on the device until you delete it. Reset removes the current library and specified local settings, and its confirmation explains what it retains. Reset preserves the Google connection, schedule, Drive backups, and local restore recovery copies. Uninstall preserves per-user app data, including the library and any saved Drive session. Remove retained local files explicitly if needed after quitting Linkme and keeping any backups you still want.

Disconnect, revoke, or delete

Choose Disconnect in Linkme's Google Drive settings to remove this device's saved Google session and stop scheduled uploads. Existing Drive backups remain. To revoke Google's grant, remove Linkme from your Google Account connections. To delete Drive backups, manage them directly in your Google Drive.

External services and sharing

  • Google handles authentication and stores backups in the Drive account you choose. The developer does not operate a server that receives your Google profile, tokens, or library snapshots.
  • Saved websites may receive a request for a favicon when a row becomes visible. If previews are enabled, deliberate hover or keyboard focus can request the saved address, including its path and query, and a supported thumbnail. Websites receive connection information such as your IP address. Turning previews off stops preview requests. Favicon requests are separate.
  • GitHub receives anonymous release-metadata requests from configured builds when a check runs. The requests can include Linkme's user agent and a cache identifier. They do not include your library, Google credentials, or an installation ID. You can turn automatic checks off.
  • Browser and email receive the selected link when you explicitly open it or choose a share action. Those services have their own privacy settings. A support email goes to the developer through email providers.
  • This website has no sign-in, forms, advertising, or analytics scripts. The hosting service can process ordinary request information such as IP addresses to serve and secure it.

Your choices and contact

You can review, correct, and remove links in the app, manage your copies in Drive, and revoke Google's access as described above. For questions or requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of information the developer holds, such as a support email, contact machekelsallemi@gmail.com. Do not send access tokens, backups, or private URLs with a support request.
